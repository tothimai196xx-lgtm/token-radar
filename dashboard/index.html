Pre-token protocols, ranked by implied value

Every protocol on the watchlist — $50M+ of TVL and no token on record — plus the early radar below the floor. Implied value is priced off the market itself: the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of live-token protocols in the same category, winsorised and shrunk toward the global median so thin categories cannot set the price. Growth counts with diminishing returns, age counts on a log scale, and a protocol that reached its TVL fast counts for more than one that took years to get there. It is a model, not a promise, and never a prediction that a token launches.

Read it as “what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is.” A token is never guaranteed, and no farm step here pays anyone to claim anything.

How implied value is computed

One function scores every row. No protocol name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The market sets the price: each category’s multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in that category, after winsorising each live ratio into [0.005, 5] and shrinking the median toward the global median (—) with weight n / (n + 4) — and a category with fewer than 5 comps can claim at most 3× the global median. Traction is the board’s own mean and spread of log TVL per day alive, so a young protocol that reached $1B fast outranks an old one that took years to get there.
